# Turn 1: The Lamp and Pick

Grey light comes off the slag dust through the common-room shutters. Carters eat fast and leave. Hob Pettigrew pours tea with his one hand and does not look up.

On the noticeboard by the door, between rank-chit offers and a lost-dog notice, hangs a sketch: a gear inside a ring. Beneath it, in a neat hand: "Discreet travelling companion for unusual mechanisms and unfinished business. Ask within."

Two of you sit at the tables. Neither has spoken yet.

**Day 0, morning. Gantry, Tallow Street.**

What do you do first?
